Removed

03Conduct and standards

On duty, every guard

  • Clean uniform and shoes.
  • Whistle and staff (lathi) at hand.
  • MyGate phone charged and at hand; know how to use it.
  • Emergency numbers at hand: hospital, ambulance, police, fire.
  • First aid box stocked at all times at P1.
  • Trained on this SOP for the SOJ site.

Dealing with residents

  • Speak politely and greet residents and visitors courteously.
  • Ask residents to follow society rules.
  • Avoid arguments. If a resident is rude, inform the secretary or chairman at once and note the vehicle and flat numbers.
  • Do not take personal work requests from residents.
  • Help elderly residents when needed.
  • Be respectful to women; keep decorum when questioning maids or housekeeping staff.

Never

  • Record videos of residents or children to capture an incident. CCTV coverage is enough.
  • Drink alcohol, sleep, or use a personal phone or headphones on duty (see Penalties).
  • Collect cash for any society charge.

04Duties by post

P1 building guard

A1, A2, A3, B1 · one per building per shift
  • Make MyGate entries for all visitors, maids and parcel deliveries.
  • Take a full round of your building 4 times per shift.
  • Keep vehicles out of no-parking areas.
  • Know the lift rescue procedure.
  • Watch for flush overflow or pipe bursts (see Water).
  • Stay alert to unusual activity.
  • Make sure cars keep to the speed limit and never drive against the one-way direction.

Rear ramp and P2 guard

Ramp from A1 to B1 · one per shift
  • Allow goods shifting from P2 only after the supervisor confirms the gate pass is in order.
  • Allow only residents with access cards to enter from P2; no guests or visitors.
  • Every hour, walk from A1 P1 to B1 P1, including the DG area, inside B1 P2 and A3 P3.
  • Watch for vehicles in no-parking spots on the ramp.
  • Only stickered vehicles park in designated areas; send badly parked vehicles out to the ramp.
  • Make sure cars keep to the speed limit and never drive against the one-way direction.

P3 entrance guard

A3 and B1 entrances · one each per shift
  • Log entry and exit of every resident vehicle.
  • Check each vehicle has a sticker and is registered on MyGate.
  • Log guest vehicles, and vehicles without stickers, against the flat and get approval.
  • Send guest vehicles to visitor parking on the rear ramp via the A3 P3 route.
  • Deliveries and maids are logged at P1. At P3, confirm they have the right address; no MyGate entry needed.
  • Make sure cars keep to the speed limit and never drive against the one-way direction.

06Visitors and deliveries

Visitor entry

  1. Visitors enter from P1 only, never from P2 or P3.
  2. Everyone (guests, couriers, postmen) is entered in MyGate with name, mobile and vehicle number.
  3. Allow entry only after the resident approves on MyGate.
  4. If no approval comes, call the flat on MyGate or intercom. Allow entry only if the owner agrees, and ask them to respond on MyGate in future.

Visitor parking

  • Visitor parking is on the rear ramp only. No visitor parking at the front or on P1, P2 or P3. To resolve: R2
  • Ask visitors to park properly and tell them about the ₹500 fine for wrong parking.
  • Vehicles picking up or dropping off may halt 10 to 15 minutes.

Parcels and delivery staff

  • All delivery staff are entered in MyGate. Call the resident first; only then let them in or accept the parcel.
  • Do not accept a parcel without the resident's approval.
  • Delivery vans (Grofers, BigBasket, Flipkart, Amazon) may halt 10 to 15 minutes in front of the P1 lobby.
  • Two-wheelers park only in the marked area.
  • Pamphlets and door-to-door marketing are not allowed. Littering or spitting: ₹500 fine per incident.

Doorstep delivery

No deliveries inside the buildings, lifts or to the doorstep, except:

  • Milk, water, newspaper
  • Gas cylinder, laundry
  • Heavy parcels

Water tankers

Tankers must never use the front ramp to reach P1. Enforce this strictly for the safety of residents, especially children.

07Domestic help and construction labour

Maids, cooks, domestic help

  • Must carry an ID card issued by Greensville main gate security. No card, no entry, even if the owner instructs otherwise.
  • Must be registered on MyGate against each flat they work in. Guards check this.
  • Check belongings when they leave; items taken out need the owner's written approval.

In-flat construction work

  • Owners email the MC before any work starts.
  • Allow workers only once the MC approves the email, and only on the dates in it. No entry after those dates.
  • Work hours: 8 am to 2 pm and 4 pm to 8 pm.
  • Workers staying overnight in a flat: security and the MC must be told in advance.

08Vehicles and parking

Registration

  • MyGate registration is mandatory for every vehicle.
  • Parking stickers are mandatory for all owner vehicles.
  • Report any vehicle without a sticker or MyGate registration to the MC at once.
  • Stop such vehicles at entry or exit and politely ask the driver to collect a sticker and register.

Enforcement

  • Watch for unauthorised vehicles entering the premises.
  • Owner vehicles parked incorrectly are locked with a jammer and fined ₹500 each time.
  • Cars must keep to the speed limit and never drive against the one-way direction.

10Silence hours · 11 pm to 6 am

  • Guards enforce silence hours; no loud music or noise.
  • Don't wait for a complaint. Call the resident and politely point out the disturbance.
  • Ask guests leaving late, and hosts of house parties, to keep noise down for the neighbours.

Cabs after 11 pm

  • Cabs carrying residents enter from P2, not P1, to avoid horns and reversing alarms at the lobby.
  • Cabs for guests still use P1.
  • Discourage horn use at all times during these hours.

12Lifts, water and plumbing

Lifts

  • Know how to switch lifts on and off.
  • Do not allow overloading.
  • In an incident, get passengers to safety first. Practise the evacuation drill.
  • When power switches from MSEB to DG, lifts stop for about 1 minute. Check nobody is stuck and help with rescue.
  • Make sure housekeeping keeps lifts clean.

Water supply

  • Know how to control the water and solar water heater supply, and where motors and switches are.
  • Know and control the domestic and flush tank motor schedules.
  • Do not change water pressure; report to FMS.
  • Report pipe bursts, cracks and leaks to the FMS plumbing team as a priority.

Flush overflow in pipes

  1. Ask the flat owner to shut the flush valve outside the bathroom window.
  2. If the flat is not known, call FMS at once.
  3. If no help arrives within 15 minutes, shut the flush line for that series.
  4. Operate valves and pressure lines very slowly and carefully.
  5. Email the MC about every incident.

13Vendors, keys and society assets

Restricted areas

Electric meter room, internet and telecom room, ducts, terraces
  • Keep locked at all times. Access only for OCS staff, security and MC members.
  • Maintain a key register (in/out) and a master list.
  • Vendors work in common areas only from 9 am to 6 pm, and only with the FMS supervisor present.
  • Report any misuse of common areas or assets by vendors to the MC at once.

Society offices

  • Security has 6 chairs and 6 tables.
  • A1 office: 4 chairs, 2 tables, 1 printer, 1 large and 1 small cupboard. The small cupboard is for security's belongings; security keeps its key. Access for security and MC only; keep it clean.
  • A2 office: for changing and keeping belongings. No cooking or tea, no footwear inside.
  • Switch off lights and taps in common bathrooms and offices after use. Security keeps the bathroom keys.

Notice boards

  • Society notices need the society stamp, a signature and an end date. Remove expired ones and hand them to the MC.
  • Promotional notices need MC approval and an end date, but no society stamp. Remove and discard them when expired.

14Emergencies and incidents

Any mishap: alert all guards on duty and in the area, including neighbouring societies TT and SV. Raise the alarm as loudly as possible. Stay alert at your post. Call the supervisor and the security company. Call the secretary and chairman by intercom or mobile.

Medical emergency / ambulance

  1. Residents share their flat number, intercom, name and mobile with security, and post on the SOJ WhatsApp group so an MC member can attend.
  2. An arriving ambulance is the top priority. Tell the other guards.
  3. Call the resident on intercom or mobile.
  4. Call and hold the service lift for stretcher access.
  5. Escort the medical team to the flat and help as needed.
  6. Keep an emergency services directory up to date.

Fire safety

  • Mock fire drill every quarter; inspect fire equipment every month.
  • Keep fire equipment working, with vendors and the FMS team.
  • All guards and supervisors trained to use fire equipment.
  • Define the rescue and evacuation protocol, and when to use parking exhaust fans and terrace smoke blowers.
  • Refuge area access and fire brigade rescue trial once a year.
  • Email the MC about every incident.

Incident handling

  • Act within this SOP.
  • Report any departure from the SOP to the supervisor, who informs the MC.
  • Escalate resident complaints to the MC immediately.
  • Avoid arguments; involve the supervisor and MC if needed.

CCTV

  • The supervisor is in charge of all CCTV equipment.
  • Make sure cameras cover all areas; keep extra watch on blind spots.
  • Keep lenses clean for clear vision.

15Reporting and communication

  • Post timestamped, located photos of guards on every round.

Send by email and keep all communication in email.

Incidents

OPENTo resolve before issue

Points where version 1 contradicts itself or is unclear. Each is marked where it appears on this page.

R1 Guest vehicles at P3

Visitor entry is P1 only (section 06), yet P3 guards log guest vehicles and route them via A3 P3 to the rear ramp. Do guest vehicles drive in through P3 after the visitor is logged at P1, or should P3 turn them back to P1?

R2 Guest parking spots

Version 1 tells guards to guide visitors to "free guest parking spots, or the ramp behind", but also says there is no visitor parking at the front or on any P level. Are there marked guest spots anywhere other than the rear ramp?

R3 2-minute round limit

Rounds are capped at 2 minutes, but P1 guards walk their whole building 4 times a shift and the ramp guard walks A1 to B1 incl. the DG area every hour. Should the limit apply only to P1 lobby checks?

R4 Night round times

Night rounds are every 2 hours, but the photo times listed are 11 pm, 2 am and 5 am. Confirm the exact times, for example 10 pm, 12 am, 2 am, 4 am, 6 am.
