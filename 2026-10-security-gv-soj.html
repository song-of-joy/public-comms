07Red flags

Seven Seas

  • Guards and supervisors paid at the unskilled floor.
  • Supervisor basic below the skilled minimum.
  • Staff labelled "caretaker", which can sidestep security-guard wage rules.
  • No gratuity; ESIC calculated on a smaller base than stated.
  • Highest cost per covered post despite the lowest wages.

K Samraat

  • GST number and PAN don't match.
  • Letterhead name differs from licensed entity.
  • No leave wages or gratuity.
  • Lady guard rate below the legal floor.

Harrier

  • No licence or contract terms sent.
  • ₹3,100/guard in non-statutory allowances; room to negotiate.
  • Ask if gratuity is billed only when actually paid.

Beta Force

  • No cost breakup or licence.
  • Silence for 15 days treated as an accepted order.
  • Auto-renewal with 10% rise.

08Before the committee decides

  1. Get the PSARA licence from Harrier, Beta Force and Seven Seas, and verify every licence on the state portal.
  2. Ask Harrier to cut optional allowances or margin; ask K Samraat to add leave wages and gratuity and quote in the licensed entity's name. Then compare again.
  3. Ask Seven Seas to re-price guards at semi-skilled and supervisors at skilled rates, and explain the "caretaker" label.
  4. Get one all-inclusive price per post: 24×7 cover, relievers, national holidays.
  5. Ask the shortlisted agencies for the last 3 months' PF and ESIC challans for deployed staff, and call two references each.
