Lift safety rules: who has to do what

A plain-English summary of the new Maharashtra lift rules, which apply from 1 November 2026. It lists the duties of residents, of the society, and of our lift maintenance (AMC) contractor. Each duty shows the rule it comes from, so anyone can check it against the original text.

01How to read the references

Rule 10(13)

Rule 10, sub-rule (13) of the Maharashtra Lifts, Escalators and Moving Walks Rules, 2026.

Act s.36(1)

Section 36, sub-section (1) of the Maharashtra Lifts, Escalators and Moving Walks Act, 2017, the parent law the Rules are made under.

Form ZI-8

Item 8 of a form attached to the Rules. Forms ZH and ZI are the official safety notices for lift landings and lift cars. Rule 10(9) makes displaying them compulsory.

Society practice

Our own good practice, not a legal requirement. It is shown separately so nobody mistakes it for law.

In March 2026 an amendment to the Act renamed the officials involved. Licences, renewals, repair orders and sealing are now handled by the Superintending Engineer, and inspections by the Electrical Inspector. The duties below are unchanged by it.

02Duties of residents, as lift users

These apply to everyone who uses our lifts: residents, family members, tenants, guests, domestic help and delivery staff.

Using the lift safely

  • Do not step in or out until the lift has stopped and the doors are fully open. Form ZI-2
  • Do not carry more than the maximum load shown in the lift. Form ZI-3
  • Do not stand in the doorway, and do not put your hand between the doors. Form ZI-4 Form ZI-5
  • Do not force the doors open while the lift is moving. Form ZI-6 Form ZI-10
  • Do not use your mobile phone while getting into or out of the lift. Form ZH-6 Form ZI-12
  • In lifts with manual gates, close both the landing gate and the car gate properly when you get in or out. Form ZI-11

Children

  • Children under 12 must not use the lift unless an adult is with them. Rule 10(13) Form ZH-5 Form ZI-8
  • Parents are responsible for making sure young children do not ride alone. Society practice

Fire and emergencies

  • Never use the lift during a fire. Use the stairs. Form ZH-2 Form ZI-7
  • If you are stuck, press the alarm button and stay calm inside. Do not try to open the car door yourself, as it is unsafe. Form ZI-9
  • Call the lift company's service number shown on the landing notice. Form ZH-7
  • Tell the security guard or the society office as well, so the fault is logged. Society practice

Respecting shutdowns and the equipment

  • Do not use a lift when the society, the operator or the lift company has put up a notice saying it must not be used. Doing so is an offence. Only repair staff are exempt. Act s.36(1) Act s.36(2)
  • Do not tamper with any part of the lift: buttons, doors, sensors, panels or the machine room. Rule 10(10)
  • Breaking the Act or the Rules can lead to a fine of up to ₹50,000. Act s.32

03Duties of the society

The law places these duties on the "owner" or "licencee" of the lift. For our lifts that is the society, acting through its managing committee. Some duties name the owner's "authorised representative". Where they do, the society can appoint one. Many of these duties rely on reports and certificates from the AMC contractor. Those are listed under Deliverables to society.

Licence, notices and insurance

  • Run each lift only under a valid working licence and its conditions. Act s.11
  • A working licence lasts 20 years. Apply to renew it at least 30 days before it expires, using Form Z and the documents listed. Rule 7(5) Rule 7(6) Act s.8
  • If the lifts' ownership changes, for example when the builder hands over to the society, apply to transfer the licence using Form ZD. If a licence is lost, apply for a duplicate using Form ZC. Rule 8
  • Display the lift licence at ground or entry level, and in the lift car. Rule 10(8) Form ZI-1
  • Put up the official safety notices on every landing (Form ZH) and inside every lift car (Form ZI). Rule 10(9)
  • Hold third-party insurance that covers the risk to passengers. Act s.23

Maintenance

  • Have a maintenance contract (AMC) with a government-approved lift contractor covering cleaning, oiling, adjusting and repairs. Act s.18 Act s.7(5) Rule 10(4)
  • Make sure the contractor services and examines every lift at least once a month. Rule 10(7)
  • Keep a log book for each lift (Form ZF). Record every repair and alteration, every breakdown other than a power cut, and any accident. The Electrical Inspector can ask to see it at any time. Rule 10(5) Act s.24
  • Fix any fault the contractor reports straight away. Rule 10(6)
  • Repair any damaged part, enclosure, gate or fastening straight away. Keep the safety gear in good order and clean. Never allow welding of broken parts that carry the load. Rule 10(15)
  • Do not stop the contractor from carrying out its duties. Rule 10(4)
  • Keep each lift to the Indian Standards (BIS) and National Building Code listed in the Rules. Rule 10(1) Rule 9(1)

Audits and government inspections

  • Through the contractor, get each lift audited and tested against the Form R checklist at least once a year, and send the report to the Electrical Inspector. Rule 10(2)
  • The government inspects each lift at least once a year and gives notice first (Form ZM). On the day, give the inspector full access, and have the contractor present if asked. Act s.17(1) Act s.14 Rule 12
  • If an order says a lift is unsafe (Form ZN), stop using it. Finish the repairs within the time given and confirm in writing once they are done. Act s.21 Rule 12(2)
  • If the order is not followed, the lift can be sealed. Act s.22 Rule 13(2)

Safety upgrades and changes

  • Every lift car must have CCTV. Existing lifts without CCTV, an Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) or an Overload Protection Device (OLPD) must have all three fitted within one year of the Rules being published. Rule 9(3) Two sub-rules are numbered (3) in the published text; this is the second
  • Do not make changes that affect safety or the lift's basic features, such as capacity, speed or number of stops, without prior written permission. Act s.10 Rule 10(3) Rule 5
  • Any lift that has been shut for 6 months or more must be tested on site by the contractor before it is used again. Rule 10(11)
  • Tell the Superintending Engineer about any lift that will be shut for more than 6 months or removed for good. If a lift is taken out of use, give notice within one month. Rule 13(1) Act s.26
  • A lift taken out of use must be removed, or disconnected from power and kept safe, with all its doors locked or barricaded. Rule 13(3)
  • Lifts may be replaced after 20 years, or earlier if the authority directs it at licence renewal. Act s.27 Act s.8(5)

Accidents

  • Report any accident that causes, or could have caused, injury or death within 24 hours to the Electrical Inspector and the local police station. Send a notice on Form ZK and a detailed report on Form ZL. Rule 11(1) Act s.15(1)
  • Stop using the lift and do not disturb anything. Restart it only with the Electrical Inspector's written permission. Act s.15(1) Form ZK
  • The society and the contractor both sign the detailed report (Form ZL). Form ZL
  • Answer the inspector's questions during any inquiry. The law requires it. Act s.15(5)
  • An owner who does not live in the building must appoint a local representative to report accidents, and tell the Electrical Inspector that person's name in writing. Act s.15(2) Act s.15(3)
  • Name one committee member as the lift contact for accident reports and inspections, and give the name to the Electrical Inspector. Society practice

Lift operators and accountability

  • If the society employs a lift operator, that person must be 18 or older, mentally and physically fit, and trained to run the lift. Rule 10(12)
  • When an association breaks the Act, the members who control its affairs can also be held personally liable. They are not liable if they show it happened without their knowledge, or that they took all due care to prevent it. For a society, this can mean managing committee members. Act s.33 Committee to confirm with legal advice
  • The licence can be suspended or cancelled if its conditions or the Rules are broken. Act s.19 Rule 10(14)

04Duties of the lift AMC contractor

The law calls the maintenance company the "approved contractor". When the society hands maintenance to it, the contractor becomes responsible for the duties assigned to it in the contract. Rule 10(4)

Licence and set-up

  • Hold a valid government licence to install and maintain lifts (Form L), and renew it at least 30 days before it expires. If renewal is late, the licence becomes void and the contractor must apply afresh. Rule 6(3) Rule 6(4) Rule 6(6)
  • To renew, show certificates from at least 10 lift owners confirming their lifts were kept in good condition. Rule 6(4)
  • Keep an office with a telephone in Maharashtra. Open an extra service station in any city where it maintains more than 50 lifts, or more than 100 km from its main office. Rule 6(2)(i)
  • Run a 24×7 phone line to take complaints and emergency calls from lift owners and respond quickly. Rule 6(2)(iii)(g)

Staff and equipment

  • Employ qualified staff: a licensed electrical supervisor, mechanical and electrical engineers, and an ITI-trained technician, each with the experience the rule sets. Rule 6(2)(iii)(a)–(d)
  • Keep at least one maintenance pair for every 50 lifts it maintains: a mechanic with 5 or more years' experience, and a helper. Rule 6(2)(iii)(f)
  • Give all staff safety gear such as safety belts and helmets. Rule 6(2)(v)
  • Keep a workshop with the required machines, and a vehicle for maintenance visits. Rule 6(2)(iv) Rule 6(2)(vi)
  • Declare that its employees do not work for any other organisation. Rule 6(1)(d)

Monthly servicing

  • Service and examine every lift at least once a month, and record the results in the lift log book (Form ZF). Rule 10(7)
  • The Form ZF monthly checklist covers the ropes or belts and sheave, the landing door locks, the brake, the governor and safety gear, the guides and rollers, levelling at each floor, the doors and their sensors, and lubrication of moving parts. It also includes a brake check on an empty car, the Automatic Rescue Device, and any repairs, breakdowns or accidents. Form ZF
  • Do the cleaning, oiling, adjusting and repairs that the contract covers. Act s.18
  • Report faults to the society so they can be fixed straight away. Rule 10(6)
  • Never weld broken load-bearing parts or the parts that support moving passengers. Rule 10(15)

Annual audit, tests and certificates

  • Audit and test each lift against the Form R checklist at least once a year, or more often if the Electrical Inspector requires it. Certify the results, and make sure the report reaches the Electrical Inspector. Rule 10(2)
  • Form R covers items such as the pit and buffers, the alarm bell, lights and fan, the car capacity notice, the doors and sill gap, the machine room, the brakes, gearbox and ropes, the limit switches, the overspeed governor, the overload trip, the fireman switch, and the log book. Form R
  • Sign the fitness certificate that goes with any licence or renewal application. In it, the contractor states it has examined and tested the lift and found it fit for use. Act s.7(6) Rule 7(2)(c) Rule 7(6)(e)
  • Test on site any lift that has been shut for 6 months or more before it is used again. Rule 10(11)

Inspections and accidents

  • Attend government inspections when the society is asked to have the contractor present. Act s.14 Form ZM
  • Sign the detailed accident report (Form ZL) together with the society. Form ZL
  • The contractor can itself be prosecuted if an accident happens because a safety feature it maintains failed. Act s.25

Deliverables to society

Documents the contractor must prepare or sign so the society can meet its own duties in section 03.

  • Monthly: a log book entry (Form ZF) for each lift after every service visit. Rule 10(7) Rule 10(5)
  • When found: a written report of any fault, so the society can fix it straight away. Rule 10(6)
  • Yearly: the certified audit and test report on the Form R checklist for each lift, sent to the Electrical Inspector. Rule 10(2) Give a copy to the society for its records. Society practice
  • For licence renewal, transfer or duplicate:
    • the lift test report (Form P), signed by the contractor Rule 7(6)(d) Rule 8(2)(e) Form P
    • the latest inspection report (Form R) Rule 7(6)(g) Rule 8(2)(h)
    • the fitness certificate Act s.7(6) Rule 7(6)(e) Rule 8(2)(f)
    • for a transfer or duplicate, if the old records are missing: the technical drawing, signed by both the society and the contractor Rule 8(2)(c)
  • After a long shutdown: results of the site tests before any lift that has been off for 6 months or more is used again. Rule 10(11)
  • After an accident: its signature on the detailed accident report (Form ZL), within the 24-hour deadline. Form ZL Rule 11(1)
  • For the safety notices: the 24×7 service phone number for the Form ZH landing notice. Rule 6(2)(iii)(g) Form ZH-7
  • At the start of the contract and on each renewal: a copy of its valid contractor licence (Form L). The society also needs a copy of the AMC itself for a licence transfer or duplicate. Rule 6(3) Rule 8(2)(j) Society practice
  • By mid-2027: a list of which lifts lack CCTV, an Automatic Rescue Device or Overload Protection, with a quote and timeline to fit them before the deadline. Rule 9(3) Society practice

06Suggested next steps for the committee

These are suggestions drawn from the duties above, not extra legal requirements.

  1. For every lift in A1, A2, A3 and B1, check that a valid working licence exists in the society's name and note its expiry date. If it is still in the builder's name, apply to transfer it. Rule 8
  2. Get a copy of the AMC contractor's current Form L licence and check its expiry date. Ask for the 24×7 complaint number in writing. Rule 6
  3. Check that the AMC promises monthly servicing, a Form ZF log book entry for each visit, and the yearly Form R audit with the report sent to the Electrical Inspector. Rule 10(2) Rule 10(7)
  4. Ask the contractor which lifts already have CCTV, an ARD and an OLPD, and get a quote and timeline for the rest, well before 28 October 2027. Rule 9(3)
  5. Confirm the society holds third-party insurance covering lift passengers. Act s.23
  6. Put up the Form ZH notice on every landing and the Form ZI notice in every lift car, together with the licence. Rule 10(8) Rule 10(9)
  7. Brief the security team: a lift that has had an accident must be stopped and left untouched, and the committee contact must be told at once so the 24-hour report can go out. Act s.15(1) Rule 11(1)
  8. Tell residents about the under-12 rule and the "do not use" notices. Rule 10(13) Act s.36
