Lift safety

For everyone using this lift: residents, guests, staff and delivery partners.

Do

  • Wait until the lift stops and the doors open fully before you step in or out.
  • Keep within the maximum load shown in this lift.
  • Keep children under 12 with an adult. They must not ride alone.
  • If this lift has manual gates, close both the landing gate and the lift gate properly.
  • In a fire, use the stairs.
  • If the lift stops between floors: press the alarm button, stay calm and wait for help.

Do not

  • Do not use the lift during a fire.
  • Do not overload the lift.
  • Do not stand in the doorway or put your hand between the doors.
  • Do not force the doors open, and never open them while the lift is moving or stuck.
  • Do not use your mobile phone while getting in or out of the lift.
  • Do not use a lift that has a "Not in use" or "Under repair" notice.
  • Do not tamper with buttons, panels, door sensors or any other part of the lift.
In an emergency

Press the alarm button. Then call the security supervisor or the society manager. Their numbers are in the MyGate app.

As required by the Maharashtra Lifts, Escalators and Moving Walks Rules, 2026.
