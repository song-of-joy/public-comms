01Scope and working hours

Areas covered

  • Buildings A1, A2, A3 and B1: floor lobbies, glass doors, elevators, common toilets, passenger and fire staircases.
  • Society office and intercom room.
  • Parking levels P1, P2 and P3, the P1 parking shed and driveway ramps.
  • Terraces, fire room, DG room, meter rooms and P1 ducts.
  • Garbage collection point and the OWC area.

Staff, hours and attendance

  • Staff strength: 12 in total, 4 male and 8 female.
  • Shift: 9 am to 5 pm.
  • The security supervisor logs housekeeping attendance in MyGate.

As set in the Security SOP v2.

Frequency at a glance

  • Daily: lobbies, glass doors, lifts, toilets, office, passenger staircase, garbage and OWC.
  • Set weekdays: fire staircase, parking levels, handrails and grills, cobwebs.
  • Set dates each month: lobby doors, P1 shed, terraces, plant rooms, fire and lobby ducts.
  • Every 3 months: deep cleaning of duct windows.
  • Twice a year: water washing of P1, P2 and P3.
  • Once a year: jet-spray deep cleaning of parking and ramps, lobby polishing.

02Cleaning standard

Electrical panels: dry dust cloth only, never wet. Clean them only with the MEP staff present to assist.

What "cleaning" means

  • Brooming, then mopping with a wet cloth and disinfectant.
  • Removing cobwebs and accumulated dirt.
  • Applies to every task on this page unless the task says otherwise.

Sanitisation

  • Glass doors, elevators and common toilets are cleaned and sanitised daily.

06Deep cleaning

Every 3 months

  • Deep cleaning of duct windows, all buildings.

Twice a year

  • Wash P1, P2 and P3 with water.
  • Routine brooming of parking levels stays at 3 times a week (see Weekly schedule).

Once a year

  • Jet-spray deep cleaning of P1, P2, P3 and driveway ramps.
  • Disc-brush polishing of lobbies.
  • Complete the whole cycle within 60 days, not more.

08Daily log and sign-off

Building tasks

  • One log sheet per week, with a column for each day and date.
  • For each task, tick off each building (A1, A2, A3, B1) as it is done.
  • Weekly and monthly tasks are marked only on their scheduled day.

Garbage and OWC

  • Mark "Work done" against each daily garbage and OWC task.
  • Note the date when manure is screened, sent away or recyclables are sold.

Sign-off

  • Name and signature of the person responsible on each sheet.
  • Record missed tasks, shortages and problems in the Notes section.

09Working with security

Points from the Security SOP v2 that involve housekeeping.

Access and keys

  • Meter rooms, telecom room, ducts and terraces stay locked. Security holds the keys and keeps the key register.
  • Security keeps the common bathroom keys.
  • Switch off lights and taps in common bathrooms and offices after use.

Checks by security

  • Guards check that housekeeping keeps the lifts clean.
  • Guards may question housekeeping staff at entry, and are required to do so with decorum.
